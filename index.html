Our Story

Italy has set the standard for fine leather goods for centuries — supple hides, careful stitching, and hardware built to last. We believe Bentonville deserves easy access to that same craftsmanship, without a trip to Milan or a big-city boutique.

bvento brings a carefully selected collection of Italian-made leather handbags to our corner of the Ozarks. Each piece is inspected by hand and offered in limited numbers, so the bag you choose feels like yours alone.
